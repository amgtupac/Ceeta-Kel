Section 02

Understanding
Your Project

Ceeta-Kel Guest House offers comfortable, welcoming accommodation for guests — whether visiting for business, leisure, or a short stay. In today's world, a well-built website is often the deciding factor between a guest choosing your property or moving on to the next.

Based on our discussions, Ceeta-Kel needs a website that:

  • Presents rooms and facilities in a warm, visually appealing way
  • Lets guests make inquiries or bookings quickly and easily
  • Builds trust and credibility with first-time visitors
  • Works beautifully on mobile phones where most searches happen
  • Is fast, reliable, and straightforward to maintain

The goal: turn every website visit into a genuine guest inquiry.

Section 04

Design Approach

For Ceeta-Kel Guest House, we will create a warm, inviting design — one that feels personal and homely while still being polished and professional. The kind of website that makes a guest feel at ease before they even arrive.

  • Warm visual tone — earthy, welcoming colors that reflect a comfortable stay
  • Photography-led — rooms and spaces front and centre to inspire confidence
  • Mobile-first — perfect on phones before anything else
  • Easy navigation — guests reach what they need in two taps
  • Strong calls-to-action — "Book Now" and "WhatsApp Us" always visible
  • Fast & lightweight — optimized images, clean code, quick load everywhere
